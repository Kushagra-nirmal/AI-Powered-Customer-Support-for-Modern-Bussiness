Learn all 6 multimodal real-time capabilities in 6 simple steps
Step 1 of 6 · Audio & Speech Real-time Hands-Free

🎙️ Full Hands-Free Voice Conversation

Speak naturally to your concierge using browser speech recognition. Silence detection automatically submits your voice query after you stop speaking, and The concierge responds instantly in a natural British AI voice.

⚡ Real-time Voice Architecture

  • Continuous Listening: Tap 🎙️ on the composer bar and begin speaking immediately.
  • Smart Silence Detection: Automatically senses a 1.3-second pause in speech and transmits the message hands-free.
  • Natural Voice Audio Synthesis: Assistant responses are spoken aloud via Web Speech API with rate and pitch tuned for clarity.
Step 2 of 6 · Visual Studio 8K High-Res GPU Render

🎨 Realtime AI Image Generation

Generate stunning 8K photorealistic artwork, 3D Octane renders, anime characters, and cyberpunk concept art in real-time. Features 1-click prompt enhancement and full-screen lightbox viewing.

⚡ Live Image Studio Capabilities

  • Prompt Fixer & Enhancer: Automatically injects volumetric lighting, 8k resolution, and photorealistic physics.
  • Multi-Style Engines: Photorealistic, Cinematic 8K, Anime/Manga, 3D Octane Render, and Dark Fantasy.
  • Interactive Lightbox: Click any rendered image to view at full resolution or download instantly.
Step 3 of 6 · Cinematic Cinema Sora & Runway Storyboards

🎬 AI Video Generation & Storyboarding

Direct high-definition cinema sequences formatted for OpenAI Sora, Runway Gen-3, and Luma Dream Machine. Includes camera movement choreography and interactive preview playback simulation.

⚡ Cinematic Video Production

  • Camera Choreography: Controls slow drone push-in, 360-degree orbital rotation, and dynamic tracking shots.
  • Simulated Playback: Interactive Play/Pause button with running timer, progress bar, and film scanline preview.
  • Production-Ready Prompts: Output prompts are formatted with aspect ratio, FPS, lighting, and physics instructions.
Step 4 of 6 · Autonomous Commerce Live Financial Ledger

🛒 Autonomous Multi-Platform Ordering

Autonomously order food, groceries, and retail products across Domino's, Swiggy, Zomato, and Amazon. The agent calculates real itemized prices, taxes, and automatically logs the order into the live tracking system.

⚡ Autonomous Checkout Breakdown

  • Itemized Receipt: Computes genuine subtotal, GST/taxes, and delivery charges.
  • Encrypted Settlement: Generates transaction ID and confirmed delivery destination.
  • Unified Registry: Placed orders automatically synchronize with the real-time tracking tool.
Step 5 of 6 · Logistics Telemetry Live Dynamic Milestones

📦 Universal Real-time Order Tracking

Track packages and food deliveries live across Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, and Domino's. Features interactive 5-step milestone progression, assigned driver details, vehicle registration, and live GPS refresh.

⚡ Real-Time Tracking Telemetry

  • Dynamic Progress: Tracks real elapsed minutes since order placement to advance delivery stages.
  • Driver & Courier Telemetry: Shows rider name, phone contact, vehicle plate, and current speed.
  • Live GPS Refresh: Click 🔄 Refresh Live GPS anytime to recalculate remaining distance and ETA.
